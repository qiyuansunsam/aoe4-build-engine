How these numbers are made
  • Gather rate per villager = C / (C/r + 2d/v + t_turn): carry C (10, 25 hunted), raw rate r from the game, walking speed v 1.125 tiles/s, one-way distance d, drop-off overhead t_turn fitted to the only published measurements (0.3 s). Techs multiply r; Golden Age tier 1 multiplies the result by 1.15 (1.225 with the Administrative wing).
  • Villagers cost 50 food (30 after Fresh Foodstuffs) and take 20 s per Town Center; a camel costs 130/45/45 and takes 35 s per stable (28 s with Military Academy, less with City Planning stacking). Research runs one tech per building at the times in the game data. Ages are the wing costs with a 100 s build; Preservation of Knowledge takes 20% off techs and wings researched after it.
  • Allocation is a deficit controller: villagers are split by what the next planned purchases still lack. The plan is the standard 3 TC opening; stables are added while every stable is busy and resources bank. Map: 16 sheep at the TC, 8 orchard bushes at 1 tile, 8 deer at 1.5, wood 1.5, gold and stone 1 tile (estimates, editable above).
  • Payback for an eco tech is the first minute at which the extra resources it produced (same villagers, with and without it) exceed its price. Military techs have no payback: they buy fighting power, not resources.
  • Simulated plan on patch 16.2.10604, not a recorded game. Not yet verified against a live match.
