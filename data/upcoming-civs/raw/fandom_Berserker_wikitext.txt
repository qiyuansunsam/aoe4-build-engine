The '''Berserker''' is an upcoming unique light [[Melee infantry unit (Age of Empires IV)|melee infantry unit]] in ''[[Age of Empires IV: Raiders of the North]]''. It is available to the [[Vikings (Age of Empires IV)|Vikings]] from the [[Barracks (Age of Empires IV)|Barracks]] starting in the [[Dark Age (Age of Empires IV)|Dark Age]]. It enrages when at low health, enabling it to heal and deal bonus damage.
