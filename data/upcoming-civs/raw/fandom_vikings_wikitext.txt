The '''Vikings''' are an upcoming [[Civilization (Age of Empires IV)|civilization]] introduced in ''[[Age of Empires IV: Raiders of the North]]''.

In the series, they previously appeared [[Vikings (Age of Empires II)|under the same name]] in ''[[Age of Empires II: The Age of Kings]]'', as the [[Norse]] in ''[[Age of Mythology]]'', and as several early modern Scandinavian [[Civilization (Age of Empires III)|states]] in ''[[Age of Empires III]]''.

== Civilization overview ==
[[File:CivPortrait-VikingsAoE4.jpg|thumb|300px|center]]
{{Quote|
The Vikings are a new civilization available with Raiders of the North Expansion for Age of Empires IV.

This civilization represents the Viking Age, a period that started with a 793 raid on Lindisfarne monastery in northeastern England and continued with people of modern Scandinavia venturing out to trade, raid, and establish colonies across Europe and beyond. The riches from raiding and trading forged the splintered jarldoms into the Kingdoms of Norway, Sweden and Denmark.

In Age of Empires IV, the Vikings civilization lives by the sword, with the Bondi Spearmen forming the shield wall for a Berserker vanguard to break the enemy line. The Longhouse bolsters Villager recruitment and provides flexible economic upgrades, allowing the early recruitment of Vikings infantry to raid an unsuspecting enemy.|Main civilization page|<ref name=":0">[https://www.ageofempires.com/games/age-of-empires-iv/civilizations/vikings/ Vikings] on ageofempires.com</ref>}}

== Strengths ==
{{Quote|
The Viking Villagers are not recruited normally, instead they are automatically recruited at no cost until the Villager capacity is reached. Longhouses increase the Villager capacity and can also be upgraded with economic attachments.

Sagas are quests that can be completed at any time to earn powerful economic or military bonuses. Seven Sagas can be completed by raiding enemy buildings, hunting wild animals, trading, and more. Plus, completed Sagas are commemorated by carved pillars near the Capital Town Center.

The Völur is a unique religious unit that can boost the production of Longhouses or can be sacrificed to establish Sacred Groves on Relics. Sacred Groves will periodically produce Berserkers, a fierce dual axe wielding infantry that will enrage at low health, healing and doing more damage for a time. Melee infantry is a particular focus of the Vikings with Bondi spearmen, Berserkers and Hirdman forming the core of the army.|Main civilization page|<ref name=":0"/>}}

== Civilization bonuses ==
* '''Living off the Land:''' Infantry can heal themselves by gathering Food from deposits such as Berry Bushes, Deer, or Boar.
* '''Northern Migration:''' Town Centers automatically recruit Villagers at no cost up to the Villager capacity (at a slower rate than standard Villagers). The Villager capacity is increased by +10 for each Longhouse constructed.
* '''Influence:''' Each Longhouse constructed within the influence of a Dock reduces the cost of all ships by 4%, up to a maximum of 16%.

== Landmarks and Age-up options ==
=== Feudal Age ===
* [[Konungr Hall]] - Periodically provides food and rallies Shield Maidens
* [[Baugr Fort]] - Acts as a Barracks. Increases Barracks' work rate and reduces their technology costs. Immediately upgrades Bondi and Berserkers.

=== Castle Age ===
* [[Valdemar Castle]] - Acts as a Keep and grants the Danevirke Wall technology. Increases the gold returned by Jylland Knights when they are killed.
* [[Gullínn Temple]] - Acts as a Sacred Groves. Allows Völur to establish Sacred Groves on Sacred Sites and unlocks Dreki's Temple Saga

=== Imperial Age ===
* [[Kongens Dør Cathedral]] - Acts as a Monastery. Increases population and maximum population by +10. Periodically generates gold for each Longhouse within its slowly expanding ability aura.
* [[Ríkets Nyckel Castle]] - Acts as a Keep that fires volleys of arrows and recruits [[Skâne Crossbowmen]] upon completion. Increases population and maximum population by +10. Reduces the cost of all unit tier technologies. 

== Unique units ==
* [[Berserker]] - Fast light infantry unit that deals high damage. Can regenerate hit points and increase damage when at low health.
* [[Bondi (Age of Empires IV)|Bondi]] - Replaces the Spearman. Has higher health and armor, and gains additional health in groups. 
* [[Drakkar]] - Unique springald ship that can throw nets on enemy ships to slow them down.
* [[Fishing Knarr]] - Unique replacement for the [[Fishing Boat (Age of Empires IV)|Fishing Boat]] that can also transport a small number of units. 
* [[Hirdman (Age of Empires IV)|Hirdman]] - Unique replacement for the [[Man-at-Arms (Age of Empires IV)|Man-at-Arms]]
* [[Jylland Knight]] - Cheaper replacement for the [[Lancer (Age of Empires IV)|Lancer/Knight]] with reduced damage and health. Returns a portion of its gold cost upon death.
* [[Longship (Age of Empires IV)|Longship]] - Unique archer ship that also acts as a transport and is improved with each onboard unit.
* [[Skâne Crossbowman]] - Cheaper replacement for the [[Crossbowman (Age of Empires IV)|Crossbowman]] with reduced damage and health.
* [[Shield Maiden]] - Heavy ranged unit that deals extra damage and has increased move speed near allied buildings.
* [[Völur]] - Support units that can boost the production of Longhouse attachments or establish Sacred Groves on Relics.

== Unique buildings ==
* [[Longhouse (Age of Empires IV)|Longhouse]] - Replaces the House. Can train Bondi be upgraded with attachments that provide different bonuses.
* [[Sacred Grove]]

== Unique technologies ==
* [[Bondi Forester]] - Each Bondi generates a passive trickle of wood.
* [[Danevirke]] - Upgrades Palisade Walls to Danevirke Walls, which have increased hit points and fire armor.
* [[Extra Oars]] - Increases the transport capacity and weapon damage of Longships.
* [[Farmathr]] - Increases Trader and Trade Ship health and movement speed.
* [[Folkekirken]] - Increases population and maximum population.
* [[Hagl Brodda]] - Archers fire two additional arrows at random when attacking
* [[Hamrammr]] - The Berserker's Berserkergang triggers at a higher health threshold and heals additional hit points.
* [[Leding]] - Causes the Dock influence bonus to also increase ship production speed for each Longhouse in its influence area.
* [[Leikr Training]] - Increase the damage, health, and range of melee infantry. Replaces [[Elite Army Tactics]].
* [[Reinforced Keel]] - Increases the health and ranged armor of all military ships
* [[Ship Barding]] - Increases the health and ranged armor of all military ships
* [[Snaerisspjot]] - Changes the Drakkar weapon to throwing spears, which have increased ranged and damage.
* [[Sunstones]] - Increases the movement speed and line of sight of ships
* [[Turnshoes]] - Increases melee infantry movement speed and charge damage.
* [[Ulfberht Swords]] - Increase the damage of Hirdmen
