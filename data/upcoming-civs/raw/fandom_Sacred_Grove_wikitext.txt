The '''Sacred Grove''' is a unique [[Building (Age of Empires IV)|building]] in ''[[Age of Empires IV: Raiders of the North]]'' available to the [[Vikings (Age of Empires IV)|Vikings]]. It can be created by a [[Völur]] starting in the Feudal Age. It increases the armor of nearby units and can regenerate hit points when no enemy units are around. Additionally, it can produce [[Berserker]]s.
