'''Ulfberht Swords''' is a unique [[Technology (Age of Empires IV)|technology]] in ''[[Age of Empires IV: Raiders of the North]]'' available to the [[Vikings (Age of Empires IV)|Vikings]] and can be researched at the [[Barracks (Age of Empires IV)|Barracks]]<!-- in the [[Imperial Age (Age of Empires IV)|Imperial Age]]-->. It increases the damage of the [[Hirdman (Age of Empires IV)|Hirdman]].
