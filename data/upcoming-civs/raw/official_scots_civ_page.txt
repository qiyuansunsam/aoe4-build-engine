The Scots
The Scots are a new civilization available with Raiders of the North Expansion for Age of Empires IV.
Scotland is a land shaped by resilience, forged through centuries of conflict and alliance. The union of the Picts and Scoti under Kenneth MacAlpin in 843 laid the foundations of the early Scottish kingdom. The Scottish Wars of Independence (1296-1357) produced heroes such as William Wallace, James “The Black” Douglas, and Robert the Bruce. The legacy of these achievements and struggles continues to shape Scotland’s traditions, culture, and identity.
The Scots field a vast roster of unique infantry that excel around Stealth Forests, seeing directly through them. Produce and protect Highland Cattle as they graze Berry Bushes for bonus Food. Raid with swift warriors or fortify your defenses with a civilization renowned for resilience, tenacity, and battlefield adaptability.
Playing the Scots
The Scots build a thriving Food economy through Highland Cattle grazing Berry Bushes. Tower Houses produce and shelter these important cattle, making them vital for the Scots economy and a prime target for enemy raids. Form the legendary Scottish schiltron with Pikemen, supported by Lochaber Axe equipped Men-at-Arms, and the fearless Gallowglass. Together these warriors create a formidable infantry force capable of holding ground and repelling attacks. Construct the Mustering Hall and begin mustering forces rapidly to respond to enemy advances. The Mustering Hall holds powerful technologies from the Lowlands and Highlands of Scotland that greatly reduce in cost when constructing a Landmark from that region. Whether you favor highland aggression, lowland defense, or a mixture of both, the Mustering Hall meets the needs of war.
The Scots at a Glance 
Cateran
The Cateran are swift light melee infantry that excel in raiding enemy workers and torching buildings. They represent a clan warrior whose rise to fame and riches are told throughout the Ages.
Gallowglass
The Gallowglass are heavy melee infantry of Norse-Gaelic descent who worked as mercenaries. Equipped with massive claymores, these fearless warriors brace against cavalry charges and deal area damage.
Tower Houses and Highland Cattle
Tower Houses act as Outposts, increase population, and produce Highland Cattle. Highland Cattle graze at Berry Bushes for bonus Food over time without depleting them.
Mustering Hall
The Mustering Hall comes with powerful unique technologies and the ability to muster warriors rapidly for Food. Add military buildings within its influence to improve the power of mustering.
Border Reiver
The Border Reiver is a light ranged gunpowder unit that excels at raiding enemy perimeters with their pistols and torches. They counter enemy ranged cavalry with ease while carefully choosing their fights.
Lochaber Axes
The Lochaber Axes technology gives Men-at-Arms an effective option against cavalry, increasing their damage and allowing them to brace against cavalry charges.

The Scots Through the Ages 
Age 1 
The Scots Dark Age begins with assigning their first Highland Cattle to Berry Bushes near a Tower House, providing their cattle with unique garrison spaces in event of a raid. In addition, Tower Houses also increase population capacity and allow for Highland Cattle production to begin. As the Scots begin to lay the foundations of their kingdom, the Pictish Pikeman stands ready to protect their village. As Scots Age up, they choose a Landmark between the Lowlands or Highlands of Scotland.     
Age 2 
Advance to the Feudal Age with the Stone of Destiny from the Lowlands and begin the feudalization process by constructing buildings within the Landmarks reach for a rebate of Gold. Produce a Kirkman and establish a Fishpond to gather Gold. While Kirkman work Fishponds, nearby Villager gather rates are also increased. Construct the Mustering Hall to unlock Lowland and Highland technologies and begin the rapid mustering of reinforcements by spending Food on muster charges.  
Age 3 
Advance to the Castle Age with the Tower of Skye from the Highlands that shrouds itself in Stealth Forests and unlocks Stealth Forest creation at Tower Houses. Maintain a defensive edge with the Scots unique ability to use Stealth Forests for vision, movement, and ambush opportunities. The fearless Gallowglass becomes available to train or muster at the Barracks and Mustering Hall. By now, the Scots have a thriving economy through Highland Cattle and clever Fishpond placements.
Age 4 
Enter the Imperial Age with Dhùn Èideann from the Lowlands to secure the region with the “Seven Sisters” cannons and increase the range of gunpowder siege engines. Research Basket-Hilted Broadswords for Caterans and witness a warrior of poor class rise to riches. Train the unforgiving Border Reivers and leverage their speed to raid enemies along the countryside. Research the final Mustering Hall technologies and secure the Kingdom of Scotland.
