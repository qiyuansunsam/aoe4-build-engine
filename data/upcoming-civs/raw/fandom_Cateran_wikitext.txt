The '''Cateran''' is an upcoming unique light [[Melee infantry unit (Age of Empires IV)|melee infantry unit]] in ''[[Age of Empires IV: Raiders of the North]]'' available to the [[Scots]]. They are described as swift units that excel at raiding workers and buildings. They represent a clan warrior whose rise to fame and riches are told throughout the Ages.
