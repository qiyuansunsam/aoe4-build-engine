The '''Longship''' is an upcoming unique variant of the [[Archer ship (Age of Empires IV)|archer ship]] in ''[[Age of Empires IV: Raiders of the North]]'' available to the [[Vikings (Age of Empires IV)|Vikings]]. It can transport units, with each passenger increasing the Longship's hit points and causing it to fire an additional arrow.
