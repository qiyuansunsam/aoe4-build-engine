The '''Longhouse''' is a unique [[Building (Age of Empires IV)|building]] in ''[[Age of Empires IV: Raiders of the North]]'' available to the [[Vikings (Age of Empires IV)|Vikings]] starting in the [[Dark Age (Age of Empires IV)|Dark Age]]. It replaces the [[House (Age of Empires IV)|House]]. In addition to providing population, it can also be upgraded with various attachments that have additional effects.
