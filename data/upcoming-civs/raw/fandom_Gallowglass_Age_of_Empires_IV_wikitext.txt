The '''Gallowglass''' is an upcoming unique heavy [[Melee infantry unit (Age of Empires IV)|melee infantry unit]] in ''[[Age of Empires IV: Raiders of the North]]'' available to the [[Scots]]. It can be created starting in the [[Castle Age (Age of Empires IV)|Castle Age]] from the [[Barracks (Age of Empires IV)|Barracks]] or [[Mustering Hall]]. It has the ability to brace against incoming cavalry, similar to the [[Spearman (Age of Empires IV)|Spearman]] and its unique variants. It can also deal [[Area of Effect]] damage.
