AoE4 Vikings: key features
Difficulty: not announced yet. This page will be updated with the in-game star rating after release.
An aggressive civilization built around early raiding and fast melee infantry.
Longhouses support the economy while strong early naval options make water maps especially interesting.
Later ages shift toward knights and trained militia, while Sagas provide long-term civilization-wide power.
Release date: October 29, 2026 (scheduled on Steam and Xbox). This is a pre-release guide.Official Steam DLC page. I am only using mechanics confirmed by official information. Exact costs, landmarks and optimized build orders will be added after the civilization is playable and enough high-level games are available to compare.
How the Vikings are expected to play
Everything shown so far points to a civilization that wants to touch the opponent early instead of sitting back. Fast infantry, raiding, an early navy and the Longhouse economy all reward tempo. The important question after release will be how much economic value is lost if the first raids fail.

The first complete build will be added after release. I will track the opening villager split, Feudal timing, first raid timing, military production and the point where top players branch into continued pressure, a second Town Center or Castle Age.
Team-game plan
For team games I will separate front-line raid builds from safer back-line economy and naval builds. The important thing will be whether Viking early pressure remains efficient on larger maps where travel time is longer.
